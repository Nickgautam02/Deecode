## Outreach & Social Media Intern

**Deecode Media House · Delhi NCR, remote considered · Paid internship**

We are an influencer marketing and talent management agency. We have 600+
creators on our roster across India, the USA and Dubai, and we have run
creator campaigns for Netflix, Airtel, Garnier, Red Bull and Ixigo, among
others. We are hiring an intern to help us find creators, talk to them,
and keep our own channels alive while we do it.

### What you'll actually do

- **Outreach.** Find the creators who fit a brief, work out who is worth
  approaching, send the DMs and the emails, and chase the ones who go
  quiet. This is most of the job and it is the part that decides whether
  a campaign happens at all.
- **Our social handles.** Plan and post for Deecode's own accounts.
  You'd be writing as us, not as you.
- **Canva.** Lay out posts, story frames and the odd deck slide well
  enough that nobody has to ask a designer first.
- **Trackers.** Keep the creator lists, outreach status and campaign
  numbers in Excel or Sheets, in a state someone can make a decision
  from.
- **Research.** Influencer and market research for campaigns we are
  about to pitch — who the audience is, who is already talking to them,
  what a category looks like before we walk into the room.

### What we're looking for

- You can start a conversation with a stranger and not disappear after
  the first "no reply".
- You write like a person. A DM that reads like a template gets ignored,
  and we can tell the difference in one line.
- You can make something look decent in Canva without a designer.
- A spreadsheet with 300 rows in it makes you curious, not tired.
  VLOOKUP and a pivot table are a plus, tidy work is the minimum.
- You are on Instagram and LinkedIn enough to have opinions about what
  works on them.

No agency experience needed. Show us a page you have run, a thing you
have made, or a list you have built, and that counts for more than a
line on a CV.

### The deal

- Paid internship. You tell us the monthly stipend you're expecting.
- Real accounts, real creators, real briefs from day one — this is not a
  shadowing role.
- Delhi NCR is a plus, because some of this is easier in a room with us.
  The work travels, though: if you're good and you're elsewhere, apply.
- Open-ended. Do it well and it turns into something longer.

### How to apply

Fill the form — it takes about two minutes and asks for your CV. That's
the whole process at this stage. Shortlisted candidates hear from us on
WhatsApp.
